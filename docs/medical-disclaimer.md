# Pharmacy Commons — Medical Information Disclaimer

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026

**Website:** [https://pharmacycommons.org](https://pharmacycommons.org)

**Operator:** Pharmacy of the Commons, LLC

Pharmacy Commons is an open educational compendium for clinical and public inquiry. It is designed to make pharmaceutical knowledge computable, auditable, and interoperable, but it is not to be used as a substitute for direct medical evaluation or the clinical judgment of a licensed practitioner.

Please read this disclaimer carefully before relying on any data, monograph, or automated output provided by the Services.

### 1. No Medical Advice or Professional Relationship

Pharmacy Commons is not a healthcare provider, pharmacy, medical practice, or substitute for professional medical judgment. Information presented through the Services is strictly for educational, informational, and research purposes.

Use of Pharmacy Commons does not establish a professional, clinical, pharmacist-patient, physician-patient, fiduciary, or advisory relationship. You should not rely solely on Pharmacy Commons to:

* Diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical condition.
* Initiate, discontinue, or modify a medication.
* Determine an individualized dose or dosing schedule.
* Make decisions involving drug interactions, contraindications, allergies, or adverse effects.
* Determine whether a medication is appropriate for a particular patient.

If you are a patient, ask your pharmacist or prescriber before changing any medication. In a medical emergency, contact 911, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at 988, or Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222 rather than relying on the Services.

### 2. Clinical and Professional Responsibility

Healthcare professionals who use Pharmacy Commons remain entirely responsible for their own professional decisions and actions. Medical evaluation, drug dispensing, and therapeutic monitoring require individualized diagnostic context and professional licensure.

Pharmacists, prescribers, and clinical teams are licensed to exercise independent judgment that cannot be outsourced to a relational database, search algorithm, or large language model. The presence of a drug, indication, dose, interaction, warning, or other data point in Pharmacy Commons does not establish that it is appropriate for any particular patient or clinical situation. The Services do not replace clinical assessment, patient-specific evaluation, professional standards of care, or institutional policies.

### 3. Primary Source Verification and Information Currency

Pharmacy Commons is committed to transparent sourcing and traceability. By anchoring therapeutic indications and safety warnings to explicit provenance, the platform links clinical claims directly to FDA document identifiers or clinical study citations.

However, pharmaceutical knowledge changes over time. Regulatory labeling, clinical evidence, safety communications, drug availability, and professional standards may change after information has been published or last reviewed. Because regulatory approvals, warnings, and black-box safety profiles are continuously updated, clinicians and users are strongly advised to verify all retrieved facts against primary scientific literature and current FDA-approved product labeling prior to making clinical decisions.

Source attribution within the database does not guarantee that the underlying source is accurate, complete, current, or suitable for a particular clinical use.

### 4. Pharmacopoe Ai and Automated Features

Pharmacy Commons may provide artificial intelligence or other automated tools, including Pharmacopoe Ai, to assist users in retrieving, organizing, summarizing, and exploring pharmaceutical information.

Architecturally, the platform is designed so that artificial intelligence functions strictly as an inspectable consumer and navigator of verified truth, rather than an ungrounded generator of medical claims. These tools operate by retrieving evidence-bearing facts and relationships from the underlying database.

Nevertheless, users must understand the following technical limitations:

* **Not Verified Advice:** AI-generated responses are not independently verified medical advice and should never be treated as authoritative clinical recommendations.
* **Potential for Error:** Automated outputs may contain errors, omissions, misinterpretations, or unsupported conclusions.
* **Mandatory Review:** Users must actively review the source records and citations linked to an AI-generated response, verifying material claims against the underlying primary source before applying them in practice.
* **Refusal to Answer:** Pharmacy Commons and its automated tools may decline to answer questions when available records do not adequately support an answer. Such a refusal does not establish that information is unavailable elsewhere or that a particular clinical conclusion is correct.
