About Pharmacy Commons

Pharmacy Commons is an open interface to structured pharmaceutical records. Information is sourced from public and federal repositories such as the FDA, WHO, NIH, and NLM, then modeled in a relational PostgreSQL database so that both human researchers and AI language models can retrieve evidence-bearing answers with clear citations.

Our mission

To unenclose pharmaceutical knowledge as a durable public trust. Since the public finances the discovery and validation of medical science, authoritative clinical evidence and drug facts should remain permanently transparent and portable for all.

What you can do here

  • Look up a medicine by its colloquial name. Each one has a stable identifier (a PCID) and a permanent address.
  • Browse by pharmacologic class, with links back to source classification systems such as RxNorm and PubChem.
  • Search and compare compendium lists, such as the most-used drugs in the US and MPJE testable drugs.
  • Use clinical tools, starting with the creatinine clearance calculator.

Every fact traces to its source

Each record links to where it came from, for example an FDA document identifier or a clinical study citation. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is shown rather than a side being chosen. Where reliable data exist, estimated environmental risk sits alongside clinical information, so prescribers can weigh it among clinically appropriate options.
